About Maya

Ten years of making people feel like themselves, only more so.

Maya trained at the Cinema Makeup School in Los Angeles before spending six years on bridal and editorial teams across the Bay Area. Her approach favors skin that still looks like skin, hair that survives the dance floor, and a kit built entirely around what actually holds up in photographs. Every appointment is private, unrushed, and built around one client at a time.

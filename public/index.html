About Amit

Ten years of making people feel like themselves, only more so.

Amit trained at the Cinema Makeup School in Los Angeles before spending six years on bridal and editorial teams across the Bay Area. His own time in front of the camera as a model taught him exactly what holds up under studio lights and camera flash — skin that still looks like skin, and a kit built entirely around what actually survives a twelve-hour shoot day.

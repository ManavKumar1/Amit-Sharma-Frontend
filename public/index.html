About Amit

Amit is an independent makeup artist and hairstylist based in San Francisco, trained at the Cinema Makeup School in Los Angeles, with ten years across bridal and editorial teams throughout the Bay Area.

Every look starts with what's already there — great makeup isn't about hiding, it's about revealing what's already extraordinary.

His own time in front of the camera as a model taught him exactly what holds up under studio lights and camera flash — skin that still looks like skin, and a kit built entirely around what actually survives a twelve-hour shoot day.
